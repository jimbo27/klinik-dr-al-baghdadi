# Write

Request: $ARGUMENTS

Before writing, settle from the request and the conversation: purpose, reader, format (email, post, article, letter, caption, page copy…), length and language. Ask one short question only if the purpose or reader cannot be inferred; otherwise state your assumption in one line and write.

Write the finished piece, ready to use:
- Open with the point, without warm-up sentences.
- Plain, specific words; concrete details over adjectives.
- Varied sentence length; no filler or stock phrases ("in today's fast-paced world", "delve", "elevate").
- The format's conventions: a subject line for emails, a headline for articles, a call to action for marketing.
- Where a fact is needed that you don't have (prices, dates, names), a clear [placeholder] instead of an invented value.

After the piece, one line offering the most useful variation (shorter, more formal, another language).
