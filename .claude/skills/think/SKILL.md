# Think

Topic: $ARGUMENTS

If the input is empty, use the most recent relevant content in this conversation; if there is none, ask one short question about what to work on.

Think the matter through, then show the thinking that matters:

1. **What is really being asked** — the goal behind the question, if it differs from the literal one.
2. **Angles** — at least three distinct perspectives (the people affected, short vs long term, cost vs benefit, the skeptic's view).
3. **Assumptions to question** — which beliefs the obvious answer rests on, and what happens if one is wrong.
4. **Second-order effects** — what follows from the likely answer a step or two later.
5. **Considered view** — your recommendation, the main trade-off you accepted, and what remains uncertain.

Go deep on the few points that decide the matter rather than wide on many.
