# Ideas

Topic: $ARGUMENTS

If the topic is too vague to make ideas specific, ask one question first.

Give 10 ideas (or the number the user asked for):
- Each: a short bold title plus one line on what it is and why it would work.
- A range: some quick and cheap, some ambitious, at least two unexpected.
- Specific to the user's situation, drawing on the conversation and the project.

Then **Top 3 picks**, one sentence each on why, and the first step for the best one.
