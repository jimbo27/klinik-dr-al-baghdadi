# Trends

Field or topic: $ARGUMENTS

Current trends need current data, so search before answering: use WebSearch (load it with ToolSearch `select:WebSearch` if needed) with several queries covering news, industry reports and data from the last 12 months.

1. **Top trends** — 5–8, each with what is happening, the evidence (figures with source and date) and why it is happening.
2. **Rising vs fading** — what is gaining momentum and what is losing it.
3. **What it means for the user** — implications and opportunities for their situation, when known.
4. **What to watch** — signals to monitor over the next 6–12 months.

Date every statistic and link every source. Say when evidence is thin or sources disagree, and label anything from memory rather than search as possibly out of date.
