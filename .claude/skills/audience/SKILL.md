# Audience

Input: $ARGUMENTS

The input names an audience and gives content, or refers to earlier content: e.g. "patients aged 60+: <text>" or "make this for investors". If the audience is missing, ask who it is for.

1. **Audience profile** in 3–4 lines: what they already know, what they want, what worries them, which language and reading level suits them.
2. **Adapted content** — vocabulary, examples, length, structure, tone and which points come first, all set for that audience. The facts stay unchanged.
3. **Changes** — the 2–3 most important changes you made and why.

When no content is given, give guidance for addressing that audience instead: key messages, tone, words to use and avoid, best channels.
