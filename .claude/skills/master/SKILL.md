# Master

Skill or subject: $ARGUMENTS

1. **Starting point** — the user's current level (ask one question if unknown) and their goal (job, hobby, exam, business).
2. **Foundations** — the 20% of concepts that unlock 80% of the skill.
3. **Roadmap** — stages from beginner to expert. For each: what to learn, a practice project, how to know the stage is passed, and rough time.
4. **Deliberate practice** — a weekly routine with a feedback loop.
5. **Resources** — kinds of resources plus specific well-known ones. Check with WebSearch before naming courses, prices or links.
6. **Pitfalls** — what usually stalls people at each stage.

Finish with the first exercise to do today.
