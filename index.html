FAQ

Common questions

What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 10am – 10pm (break 2pm – 4pm). Closed on Saturday and Sunday.
Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. You can also WhatsApp us to book a slot.
Which panels do you accept?
PMCare, CompuMed, Great Eastern, RedAlert, Fomema, Peka B40, PERKESO, LHDN and 7-Eleven. Please bring your IC and panel card.
Do you treat children?
Yes. We see patients of all ages, from babies to seniors.
Where are you located?
Level 1, Menara A (Akasia), Youth City Residence, Putra Nilai.
