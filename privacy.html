Privacy Policy

How we look after your personal data.

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Klinik Dr. Al Baghdadi ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this website, our social media accounts (including TikTok and our Google Business Profile), and the internal tools we use to manage them, including our TikTok-connected app "Klinik Al Baghdadi Agent". It is provided under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). A Malay version follows below.

1. Who we are

Klinik Dr. Al Baghdadi, a family GP clinic in Putra Nilai.
Phone / WhatsApp: +60 10-343 4711
Email: info@klinikalbaghdadi.com
Address: L1-5A, Menara A (Akasia), Youth City Residence, Jalan Bandar Belia 1/1, Bandar Wawasan Putra Nilai, 71800 Nilai, Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia

2. Patient medical records

Medical records and other information you give us as a patient are kept under medical confidentiality rules and are handled separately from this website and our marketing tools. We never use patient medical information for marketing, and we never upload it to social media or to the tools described in this policy.

3. Information we collect

  • When you contact us (WhatsApp, phone, email): your name, phone number or email address, and what you write to us.
  • When you visit this website: basic technical data (such as browser type, pages viewed and approximate location) collected through cookies by Google Tag Manager and related Google measurement tools, used in aggregate to understand visits.
  • Public reviews and comments: we read reviews and comments you post publicly about us (for example on Google) so we can reply. In our replies we never confirm that anyone is our patient and never mention health details.
  • Our TikTok app ("Klinik Al Baghdadi Agent"): this app is used only by the clinic, for the clinic's own TikTok account. With the clinic's authorisation it accesses that account's basic profile information (account ID, display name and profile picture), the list of the clinic's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments and shares), and permission to upload videos that the clinic owner has approved to the clinic's own account. It does not access personal data of TikTok viewers or of any other account.

4. How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and arrange visits.
  • To improve our services, website and health information.
  • To prepare, review and publish the clinic's own posts and videos. Every item is approved by the clinic owner before it is published.
  • To understand how our posts perform, using the public statistics of our own videos.

5. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our website and tools: Cloudflare and GitHub (hosting and security), Google (Business Profile and website measurement), TikTok (our TikTok account), Telegram (internal notifications to the clinic owner) and AI service providers that help us draft marketing text. We do not send patient medical information to these providers. We do not sell personal data.

6. Sharing

We share personal data only with the providers above for the purposes in this policy, or when the law requires us to.

7. Security and how long we keep data

Access to our tools is limited to the clinic. Access keys (such as the TikTok connection) are stored as encrypted secrets. We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it. The clinic can disconnect the TikTok app at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); its stored access keys are then deleted.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to access or correct your personal data, withdraw consent, or limit how we use it. Contact us using the details in section 1. We will reply within 21 days.

9. Children

This website is for general information. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it; parents or guardians may contact us on a child's behalf.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.

2. Rekod perubatan pesakit disimpan di bawah peraturan kerahsiaan perubatan, berasingan daripada laman web dan alat pemasaran kami, dan tidak sekali-kali digunakan untuk pemasaran atau dimuat naik ke media sosial.

3. Data yang kami kumpul: butiran yang anda beri bila menghubungi kami (nama, nombor telefon atau e-mel dan mesej anda); data teknikal asas lawatan laman web melalui kuki Google Tag Manager; ulasan dan komen awam tentang kami (dalam balasan, kami tidak mengesahkan sesiapa sebagai pesakit atau menyebut butiran kesihatan); dan, untuk aplikasi TikTok kami yang digunakan oleh klinik sahaja bagi akaun TikTok klinik sendiri: maklumat profil asas akaun itu, senarai video klinik serta statistik awamnya, dan kebenaran memuat naik video yang telah diluluskan oleh pemilik klinik. 7. Hak anda: di bawah PDPA anda boleh meminta akses atau pembetulan data peribadi anda, menarik balik persetujuan atau mengehadkan penggunaannya. Hubungi kami melalui butiran di atas. Kami akan membalas dalam masa 21 hari.

8. Perubahan: dasar ini mungkin dikemas kini. Tarikh berkuat kuasa di atas menunjukkan versi terkini.
